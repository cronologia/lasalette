# KEYWORDS — naming variants for searching sources and corpora

A finding aid, not an assertion: listing a term here does not claim it appears
anywhere. Read this before sweeping any corpus or source for the subject
(sourcing-rules: "Absence is a claim, and needs its own evidence" — pair every
zero with a positive control).

## The place and the event

- La Salette (the common short form — also matches the two congregations)
- La Salette-Fallavaux (the commune, Isère; official spelling)
- La Salette Fallavaux (unhyphenated variant)
- la Salette / LaSalette / La-Salette (case and hyphen variants seen in older
  and English-language texts)
- Notre-Dame de La Salette (fr)
- Nossa Senhora de La Salette / Nossa Senhora da Salette (pt)
- Nuestra Señora de La Salette / Nuestra Señora de la Saleta (es — "la Saleta"
  is an older Hispanized form)
- Our Lady of La Salette (en)
- Beata Maria Virgo a La Salette / vulgo "de la Salette" (la — the 1915 AAS
  decree writes "Beatissimam Virginem sub titulo Reconciliatricis vulgo de la
  Salette")
- Reconciler / Réconciliatrice / Reconciliatrix / Reconciliadora (the shrine's
  distinctive Marian title)

## The secret(s)

- Secret de la Salette (fr — the form used by the 1915 Holy Office decree:
  "vulgo dictum «Secret de la Salette»")
- Secret(s) of La Salette (en)
- Segredo de La Salette (pt) / Secreto de La Salette (es)
- "L'apparition de la très Sainte Vierge sur la (sainte) montagne de la
  Salette" (title of Mélanie's 1879 Lecce booklet and its 1922 reprint — the
  1923 Index decree cites the longer "sainte montagne" form)

## Persons

- Mélanie Calvat / Melanie Calvat / Mélanie Calvat-Mathieu / Sœur Marie de la
  Croix / Maria della Croce (her religious name; Italian sources)
- Maximin Giraud / Maximin Giraud dit "Mémin" / Pierre-Maximin Giraud
- Philibert de Bruillard (Bishop of Grenoble; also "de Bruillard",
  "Mgr Bruillard")
- Jacques Ginoulhiac (his successor)
- Salvatore Luigi Zola (Bishop of Lecce; also "Mgr Zola")
- Prospero Caterini (Holy Office cardinal, 1880 letter)

## Terms known to mislead

- "La Salette" alone also matches the Missionaries of La Salette (M.S.), the
  Sisters of La Salette, and dozens of parishes and schools worldwide — a hit
  is not necessarily the apparition or the commune.
- "Salette" appears in Italian confraternity names ("della Salette", e.g. in
  the 1915 AAS volume an unrelated Montefalcione sodality) — verify context
  before counting.
